GPT-6 Astra Can Design Robot Experiments Itself, Letting AI Train AI

When general-purpose language models can directly debug and train their own robot strategies in simulation, the valuation narrative of independent embodied-AI companies—‘physical AI beats digital AI’—starts to crack.

Robot researchers split into three camps over Astra's impact

At an IROS dinner, CMU assistant professor Shi Guan Ya conducted a survey of roughly 100 roboticists, asking how Astra affected their views. Responses splintered into three camps: one believed general-purpose models solve everything—‘robotics is over’—making further research pointless; a middle faction saw general-purpose and physics models as two separate layers, each occupying its ecological niche; a third, radical group insisted general-purpose models would never solve today's specific robotics problems. Chen Zhe estimates the ‘solve everything’ camp represents about a third of respondents—a notable split at a conference historically focused on classical robotics like control and tactile sensing.

— Chen Zhe
20:26

Astra's most disruptive capability: designing and testing its own experiments

Astra operates at three levels. First, it acts as a direct policy model outputting end-effector positions. Second, it supervises and corrects VLA model outputs—Yinhe General's data showed Astra alone succeeds 26% of the time, but when supervising π0.5's output, success rates reached 48%. Third, most striking: Astra autonomously builds simulation environments in Blender, generates objects and properties, then debugs motion-control strategies—completing policy modeling, training, and optimization end-to-end. This is ‘physical RSI’: AI training AI in the physical world.

— Chen Zhe
27:34

Astra's spatial breakthrough was built on thousands of Blender projects

The spatial-reasoning leap stemmed primarily from data, not new methods. In May, the team hired large numbers of Blender modelers for large-scale trajectory annotation—explaining Astra's jump in Blender generation capability. Such costs amortize across multiple uses: 3D assets, video, robot training. General-purpose companies have structural advantage here. The field suspects egocentric first-person video in pretraining, though unconfirmed; from scaling-law logic, Chen Zhe sees no reason to exclude it.

— Chen Zhe
39:46

Embodied startups survive by becoming reusable interfaces for general models

If general-purpose models handle task decomposition and spatial reasoning, what's left for embodied startups? Chen Zhe advises: become the reliable ‘interface’ general models repeatedly call, while defending true closed-loop advantages—tactile and force feedback, domain constraints, hardware reliability, failure recovery, and accumulated industry data. He compares automotive supply chains: Tier-1 suppliers wedged between OEMs and component makers get squeezed from both sides.

— Chen Zhe
46:52

Fei-Fei Li's World Labs sale shows the exit path for Physical AI startups

World Labs was acquired by AMD for $8.2 billion in AMD stock. Li's team had built spatial-reconstruction model Atlas and acquired simulation company SceniX. The price may not reflect pure business value, but signals what a $1 trillion company will spend on Physical AI strategy. It warns other founders: the best near-term exit is likely acquisition by a large tech company, since such buyers typically value only the first target. Chen Zhe emphasizes China lacks comparable acquisition pathways.

— Chen Zhe
56:56

Unitree's stock collapse belies the strengthening case for quality hardware

Unitree debuted at a peak exceeding 440 billion yuan but fell 60%, though still above its 600+ billion IPO valuation. Chen Zhe attributes volatility to short-term capital in the new-stock float; real valuation emerges after August 2026 when ChiNext lockups lift and institutional money reprices. On fundamentals: H1 2026 revenue grew 48% YoY, with humanoid output rising. His logic: Astra proves general models accelerate physical-AI deployment, which amplifies demand for reliable hardware. Unitree resembles Nvidia in autonomous driving—stronger upper-layer models drive greater hardware-ecosystem value.

— Chen Zhe
1:11:07

Showing a video now teaches robot skills faster than days of fine-tuning

In-Context Learning is the new capability Gen1.5 and S1 showed this quarter: without parameter retraining, a few-second-to-few-minute demonstration video lets models learn new tasks, replacing hours or days of teleoperation plus fine-tuning. Strikingly, this transfer works on out-of-distribution tasks never seen during training and can compose multiple learned atomic skills into longer sequences. The two companies differ on implementation: Generalist uses shorter trajectories and first-person egocentric input with joint data; Skill AI emphasizes third-person video up to ten minutes, suited for full-body skill composition.

— Chen Zhe
1:29:18

FCC regulations now check robot imports by origin of core components

In July, the US FCC expanded certification scope to robots over 2 kg with mobile sensing, networking, and control—including vacuums and lawn mowers—tallying foreign component origin at the core-part level. A US-brand robot with 65% or more parts from China or third countries faces rejection. Unitree's current models like G1, R1, and H2, already certified, face no immediate impact, but new designs will encounter friction. Chen Zhe reads this as tariff-driven policy unlikely to disrupt decades of Chinese supply-chain depth; long-term solutions will likely mirror automotive: moving production overseas.

— Chen Zhe

Glossary

In-Context Learning (ICL)
Learn new tasks from a few-second-to-few-minute demonstration video without retraining model parameters.
Physical RSI
AI autonomously builds simulation environments, trains its own strategies, completing the feedback loop—AI training AI in the physical world.
VLA
Embodied policy model that directly outputs motor-control signals for robots based on visual input.
World Action Model (WAM)
Action model using video generation as backbone, minimizing reliance on the language-understanding layer.
Robot Dojo
Open-source embodied-task benchmark used to compare success rates across different policy models.
FCC
US Federal Communications Commission; now certifies robots with mobile sensing, networking, and control.
