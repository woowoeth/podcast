Coding Is AGI's Second Act: No Leading Coding Model Is Like No Leading GPU

The real dividing line for model companies isn't consumer DAU — it's whether you can organize a few hundred of the smartest people to go all in on the dirty work of data. Anthropic wins on strategic focus; OpenAI loses by wanting everything.

3:04

The past quarter's progress exceeded all of 2025

Guangmi reduces the past quarter's most essential turning point to Anthropic's leap from Opus 4.5 to 4.6, which he considers an epochal improvement on the order of GPT3 to GPT4 — the model went from ‘can converse and answer questions’ to ‘a real agent that can do high-value tasks.’ Because task value rose, usage and value rose together. His felt sense is that the past quarter's model progress may have exceeded all of 2025's progress, and the acceleration is especially fast — there may be another GPT3-to-GPT4 leap before June or July this summer.

— Guang Mi
6:04

Frontier lab researchers no longer write code

Guangmi describes the real felt sense on the Silicon Valley front line: AI researchers at frontier labs and very strong programmers basically don't write code anymore. Last year a system might still have 70-80% human-written code; this year it may be less than 1%, and the daily work is ‘AI writes, humans review’ — and even human review capacity isn't enough. People he knows spend several hundred dollars of tokens a day, several thousand a week. The effect: going from an idea to working code used to take two or three weeks; now it's one or two days. The more qualitative signal: many AI research breakthroughs aren't brought by human engineers but by Codex and Cloud Code — AI today can significantly accelerate AI.

— Guang Mi
9:07

One or two million tip users contribute more revenue than fifty or sixty million subscribers

Guangmi points out that Anthropic officially announced AR exceeding OpenAI, but more importantly the one or two million users at its tip contribute more revenue than OpenAI's maybe fifty or sixty million subscribers. This means the competitive metric has changed: no longer just chasing DAU or ad scale, but chasing Token Usage, especially super developers or tip users. He judges Coding's explosiveness is even steeper than ChatGPT's was; if the momentum continues, by year-end OpenAI and Anthropic's AR could reach $80 billion or $100 billion, and next year head toward $200-plus billion.

— Guang Mi
12:10

Model companies that don't prioritize Coding will drop out of the first tier

Guangmi's second strong view: if a leading model company doesn't prioritize Coding, it will most likely drop out of the first tier, and there's no such thing as a Coding Model only for internal use, because your own task data distribution isn't comprehensive enough — you'll definitely fall behind. More critical is the risk of supply cutoff — once you reach the first tier and threaten Anthropic, Anthropic will most likely cut you off; OpenAI was cut off too, xAI was cut off too, probably most of Google was cut off, and Meta may be cut off someday. So Coding is like GPU: no leading Coding model is like no leading GPU — using A100 versus others using GB is a huge difference.

— Guang Mi
17:15

The hard part of doing Coding well is organization and data, not technology

Guangmi believes doing Coding well comes down to two things: organization and culture, and data. Coding isn't just a technical know-how problem; it's more a strategic and organizational problem. The difficulty is which AI lab can organize the smartest few hundred people to go all in on the single thing of Coding — because there's a lot of dirty and hard work in it, and the smartest people in every lab want to make their own bet, do 0-to-1, and become Elia. What's different about Anthropic is that the founders realized from the start that data is the root of all problems; valuing data is in their bones, and there's a rumor that chief scientist Jared Kaplan personally leads data negotiations.

— Guang Mi
22:19

Anthropic is a victory of strategy, not technology

Guangmi reviews Anthropic: going all in on coding wasn't clear on day one either — on one hand there was no opportunity left on the consumer side, and on the other, releasing Sonnet 3.5 in summer 2024 gave positive feedback on coding. After that they went all in on one thing: coding, giving up consumer and giving up multimodality. Strategically it's very top down, while OpenAI is especially bottom up. Anthropic's founders are honest and technically savvy, leading everyone to do data and engineering, the team is stable, and everyone believes in AGI. They don't mythologize any single link — pretraining-hits-a-wall theory, RL-as-god theory, reasoning models — they didn't follow any of the trends, more like a collective, a team, an industrialized system.

— Guang Mi
40:37

OpenAI has a 50% chance of being AGI's final winner

Guangmi isn't so pessimistic about OpenAI, believing there's a 50% chance the ultimate AGI winner may still be OpenAI. The logic: today's secret to victory may be the next era's poison — OpenAI's excessive success with ChatGPT made them focus on 2C and neglect Coding, and because they had to care about Inference cost, the model was never made very large. Anthropic's success is more gradual accumulation, doing details well — a victory of execution, focus, or organization — but it may not withstand OpenAI's next paradigm-level breakthrough. OpenAI's bottom-up culture opens up free exploration; today its Coding capability is very strong, and one or two people can do earth-shaking things.

— Guang Mi
1:07:51

Human knowledge and intelligence have become cheap

Guangmi on social impact: the most essential point is that human knowledge and intelligence have become cheap. Previously, acquiring knowledge through study and reading could get you a job, but today that intelligence and knowledge are all in the model, greatly compressed into compute resources or tokens. He judges that maybe 70-80% of people's value and meaning in society will undergo subtle changes. AI will also bring major deflation — after using ChatGPT and Cloud, demand for consultants and other software has shrunk a lot; one product satisfies many needs, and in the long run many SaaS will disappear too. This year is definitely a painful year when humans begin facing unemployment; maybe 30% of jobs will feel gone.

— Guang Mi

Glossary

Harness Engineering
The engineering of building work environments and managing constraints for Agents, so that ordinary models can also do high-value tasks.
Token Usage
A measure of the total tokens a model consumes; Guangmi believes it reflects a model company's value better than DAU.
Solta model
Guangmi's term for the most leading frontier model; continuously doing it well is the key indicator for investing in model companies.
feedback loop
The cycle from task execution to receiving feedback; the shorter the loop, the faster AI progresses — which is why Coding runs out first.
