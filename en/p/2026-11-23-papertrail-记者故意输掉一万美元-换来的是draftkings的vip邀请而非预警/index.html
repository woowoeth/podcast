The system treated problem gambling as a VIP opportunity

A ProPublica reporter deliberately mimicked problem-gambler betting patterns in an experiment. DraftKings' VIP system responded by treating his losses as a sign of a ‘valuable customer,’ sending responsible-gambling warnings and bonus promotions to his phone almost simultaneously.

Loss chasing, not losses alone, signals problem gambling

Jake followed expert guidance to mimic a problem gambler: when the Knicks fell behind, he cleared his account to bet a long-odds Brunson points prop, then deposited again overnight into Cavaliers and Nuggets bets, escalating from $25 weekend wagers to losing nearly $1,800 in one night. Experts flagged the pattern—repeated deposits while chasing losses, climbing odds—as the industry's red flag for problem gambling, not the total amount lost.

14:19

One night of problem gambling triggered an immediate VIP invite

Within 24 hours of this textbook problem-gambling session, Jake received a DraftKings VIP showcase email, entering a three-week trial. The company was rewarding the exact behavior from the night before—repeated deposits, chasing losses overnight, all money gone—by promoting him from a casual $20-weekend player to a ‘valuable customer.’

— Jake Pearson
17:29

VIP advancement rewards the lowest-probability bets

The VIP program's fine print stated that lower-odds parlays tier up fastest. Psychologist Grubbs explained that parlays link to problem gambling because ‘the odds look enticing but the probability of winning is terrible.’ Anthropologist Schull added that micro-betting—where seconds elapse between wager and result—produces the same flow state as slot machines, making it hardest to stop.

20:37

Bonuses arrived hours after problem-gambling warnings

After consecutive losses, Jake received a responsible-gambling video reminder and dismissed it. Within hours, he got a targeted promotion for exactly these long-odds parlays, internally labeled ‘boosts don't stop.’ He said the push ‘incentivized me to bet more.’ The reminder and reward were near-simultaneous, not sequential.

— Jake Pearson
23:39

VIP hosts approved larger bonuses right after cool-off ended

Jake initiated a three-day cool-off period. When his account reactivated, the prefilled deposit amounts had dropped from $500–$900 to $20–$50, suggesting the system was tightening. But when Jake then proposed depositing $5,000 and requested a deposit match, his VIP host immediately agreed—despite the extreme self-restriction that had just ended.

— Jake Pearson
33:54

A homeless gambler reached diamond tier while in acute crisis

Problem gambler Joe Early described stealing, sleeping on the street, and suicidal thoughts while his VIP tier climbed to diamond—the second-highest level. He mentioned that hosts had hinted at certain ‘trigger words’ (like mentioning unpaid rent or crisis) that would force the company to intervene. He even asked hosts what these words were to avoid being restricted.

— Joe Early
41:02

DraftKings said every interaction was working as intended

When Jake described chasing losses, bonuses after warnings, and post-cool-off enticements, DraftKings' responsible-gambling officer Lori Kalani replied that ‘the system operated the way it should’ and noted that the company sent Jake 32 responsible-gambling messages during that period, which were ‘appropriate to his account activity.’ She rejected the idea that the experiment drew any broader conclusions.

Regulatory pressure led DraftKings to close the account

As three Congress members inquired into the VIP program, Louisiana's gaming regulator delayed renewing DraftKings' license, and Ohio considered banning promotions within 48 hours of users enabling responsible-gambling tools, Jake asked DraftKings about regulatory risks. The company then closed his account, returning only the $0.94 remaining balance.

Glossary

loss chasing
Betting more money immediately after a loss to recover the amount lost.
parlay
A single bet combining multiple game outcomes; all must win for a payout, with high odds but extremely low probability.
micro bet
Ultra-short-duration bets on sub-match events (e.g. next basket) with seconds between wager and result.
cool-off period
A self-imposed voluntary restriction period in which a user cannot wager or deposit.
VIP host
A platform representative assigned to high-value users, providing personalized promotions and account management.
