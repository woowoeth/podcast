Executive Order Says Vaccines Are Like Soda, Splits MMR Into More Shots: Every Sentence Is a Lie

In the "gold standard" executive order, every sentence is called a lie: splitting MMR into more shots, vaccines as soda, Merck refusing — and the new CDC director will face a loyalty test.

The argument · tap a timestamp to hear it

2:03

The executive order is not a scientific assessment; every sentence is a lie

The August 10 executive order claims that "authentic nations maintain high vaccination rates through public trust and education, while US states rely on school mandates." The conversation immediately dismantles this framing: this is not a scientific assessment, "every sentence in it is a lie," and "gold standard" is just a label. There are counterexamples from the COVID period of countries that got people lining up for vaccines through education investment; but the 50 US states differ enormously, and vaccination rates cannot be maintained on trust and education alone.

9:15

Splitting MMR has no scientific rationale, it just means two more shots

The order "recognizes" that measles, mumps, rubella, diphtheria, tetanus, pertussis, polio and others should be recommended for all children, yet demands that MMR be split into three single-disease vaccines, with each shot given at as separate a visit as possible. The key judgment: this increases the number of shots and visits, with no scientific rationale, and no manufacturer is willing to do it. Trump's stated reason on signing was that combination vaccines contain a "dangerous amount of liquid," saying one shot is like a bottle of soda; in reality most vaccines are only about 0.5 milliliters. Merck's response was 10 years and hundreds of millions of dollars, and a clear no.

14:30

Vaccination has always been a state power; the executive order overreaches

US vaccination decisions have historically rested with the states, with the federal government providing ACIP recommendations and the CDC director signing off; the executive order is an overreach. This is the third vaccine-related executive order in less than a year, the previous one was already blocked by a judge, and this one will most likely follow the same path. The new CDC director previously said "they will never ask us to put children at risk"; now that the order is in front of her, how she responds becomes the first test. HHS is also soliciting members for the National Vaccine Advisory Committee, and someone joked that getting selected means having to stop doing the clinical update.

21:45

Cyclospora season isn't over, and the risk sits on one production line

The CDC has received 13,895 laboratory-confirmed Cyclospora cases, with at least 10,455 more pending further investigation; Michigan alone accounts for 12,485 cases and 279 hospitalizations, with deaths already reported. Missouri has 1,577 cases, New York City 594. August should be near the end of the import season, some sources have been identified and related products pulled from shelves. The risk remains that 40% of US lettuce comes from Taylor Farms, and contamination on the Mexico production line is the main concern.

23:51

Screwworm deaths come not from the worm but from secondary sepsis

The US has had 45 screwworm cases, concentrated in New Mexico and West Texas. Mexico reported 204 cases from April 2022 to March 2026, 202 analyzable: 72% male, mean age 61, 55% on the lower limbs, 77% with at least one comorbidity, and of 6 deaths only 1 was directly attributed to screwworm. The larval stage is an obligate parasite, eggs hatch in 12-24 hours, larvae feed on living tissue for 5-7 days before dropping off; the mechanism of death is that in neuropathic patients a wound is eaten open and bacteria enter the bloodstream, causing sepsis.

28:58

The county RFK once lectured became the measles epicenter

Measles has reached 2,484 cases this year, with 10%-20% hospitalized and deaths already reported. The New York Times dug up that five years ago in Lancaster, Pennsylvania, RFK told the Amish community that the CDC and other agencies "can't stand you being healthy," and joked that the treatment for measles is chicken soup plus vitamin A. Now the same county has become one of the largest outbreak sites in the country, with the state reporting 230+ cases, and physicians believe it is severely undercounted. The conversation's stance is clear: hundreds of children hospitalized and gasping for breath is not something to be discussed lightly.

40:22

Get a monoclonal antibody first, then a vaccine, and the B cell response is suppressed

A JID study asked: in people who received a COVID monoclonal antibody during the acute phase, is the immune response to a later mRNA vaccine altered? Analysis of 43 people showed that at day 140, neutralizing antibody titers were lower in those who had received the monoclonal antibody than in the placebo group; RBD-specific memory B cells were reduced at both day 56 and day 140, but spike-specific CD4/CD8 T cells were unaffected. The conclusion is that anti-SARS-CoV-2 monoclonal antibodies limit the RBD-directed B cell response to a subsequent vaccine, without harming T cells. The unanswered question Griffin cares about is whether vaccinating at 3 months or 6 months can avoid this suppression.

53:59

The 2 on the wastewater chart has no units, it is not a concentration

A listener asked what the "2" on the CDC wastewater chart is. The answer gives the full pipeline: sampling → concentration → nucleic acid extraction → qPCR or dPCR to get a concentration such as gene copies per liter → normalization for rainfall and sewage composition → conversion by service population to gene copies per person per day → finally a 7/15-day rolling average and regression fit. But Vincent stresses that the "2" itself has no units, it is just a categorical score. Looking at the number alone leads to misreading; it must be read alongside hospitalization and emergency department trends.

Glossary

MMR
The three-in-one measles-mumps-rubella vaccine given routinely in childhood; the executive order wants to split it into three shots.
ACIP
A CDC committee that sets US vaccine recommendations, which are then approved by the CDC director.
lyophilized
Dehydrating a vaccine into a powder so it needs no cold chain and stays stable at higher temperatures.
PICO
An evidence-based-medicine question structure defining population, intervention, comparison and outcome.
sterile insect technique
Releasing sterile males so that females, after a single mating, produce no offspring, suppressing the target insect population.
RIG
Rabies immune globulin, providing passive neutralizing antibodies for about 7 days before the vaccine takes effect, used for post-exposure prophylaxis.
