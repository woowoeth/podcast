AI Application Startup: A General Agent Can't Eat Verticals, and the First Year Nearly Killed Us

Chen Mian's post-mortem: the first-generation product won the subsidy war but was taken down, leaving only 4,000 yuan in the bank; the opportunity for vertical agents lies in general models being unable to digest industry data and interactions—provided AGI doesn't arrive within five years.

7:18

The peak is during the heat of battle; afterwards it's a mess

Chen Mian experienced two battles at Didi and Mobike, and found that during the fight is the highlight of the business model, but after the fight, the model itself isn't that good. Mobike looked like a big business of 40-50 million rides, but that was brought about by unlimited supply and unlimited supply costs—users weren't charged, bikes didn't make money, and losses were terrible. He concluded: if supply is limited and has a cost, it's impossible to maintain such a large scale; to break even, users will definitely become more expensive and bikes will definitely decrease, and the business might only do a few million rides a day, with low average order value and low gross margin—neither profitable nor useful. So you still have to respect the essence and common sense of business.

— Chen Mian
37:43

Burned two million US dollars in three months, then got taken down

From June to September 2023, Chen Mian went all in on the subsidy war, subsidizing creators, users, and poaching talent, burning over two million US dollars in three months. The first-generation product became number one in China in the image generation track, with over one million monthly active users. But in September it was taken down. The good news was that all competitors were also taken down; the bad news was that the money was gone, with only a few million RMB left in the bank, and the team was cut from fifty or sixty people to thirty. He reflected: the strategy was right, the business was right, and the competition was won; the mistake was that in his first startup he had no experience in cash flow management and no judgment on funding节奏—he didn't raise when he should have, and when he shouldn't have, no one would invest no matter who he asked.

— Chen Mian
44:09

Only 4,000 yuan left in the bank, he paid out of pocket to last two more months

After being taken down, funding was extremely difficult. Chen Mian met three to four groups of investors every day, talked for over an hour each, spoke for five or six hours, and saw all institutions but no one invested. At the most desperate point, the team was leaving every day, and investors kept asking a bunch of questions, finally saying ‘If you're not listed, we won't invest.’ He calculated that the company had only 4,000 yuan left in the bank, but at that time the team was only ten to twenty people, each taking only 10,000 yuan, some not taking money, labor costs were over 100,000 yuan a month, and revenue was almost break even. He said if that investor didn't invest, he would pay out of pocket for two months to hold on, waiting for the license to come down in April. He never felt the company would fail, because he knew why it had come to this point, and he knew what he had done wrong and what he had done right.

— Chen Mian
45:17

Customer acquisition cost three to four yuan, ad spend is just a pipeline

Chen Mian said acquiring a user is now extremely expensive, but the first-generation product's customer acquisition cost was very low, only three to four RMB, because at that time Chinese users needed this thing, and they were the best. He emphasized the importance of the time window: when Kimi first started advertising, there were few AI advertisers on Bilibili, so of course it was cheap; later when Doubao, Yuanbao, etc. flooded in, it became expensive. Ad spend is a pipeline, a stimulus, not the driving force of customer acquisition—when people know you, the cost of advertising to them is low; when they don't know you, pushing ads is expensive. So the core of brand-performance integration is the timing of the product, and timing comes from innovation and the time of entry.

— Chen Mian
55:58

Canvas plus dialog box, waiting for models' agentic capabilities to strengthen

Lovart's pre-research was divided into two parts: the preparation of the canvas started early, in the second half of 2024 when the company came back to life, because they judged that the future intermediate form would be a canvas. But agent capabilities take time. At first they wanted to do workflow, letting users build nodes on the Canvas, but found that designers don't talk logic, they talk feeling, and building nodes simply didn't work. Until around December they found that Cloud 3.5 worked very well, and the model's agentic capabilities became stronger, and they thought of using AI to help designers plan workflows, greatly lowering the threshold and rapidly improving the experience. Thus the form was outlined: the canvas is the table, the dialog box is the person, the toolbox is the tools, plus an editor. From idea to execution took about three to four months.

— Chen Mian
1:01:47

Racing to be the world's first vertical agent, first-mover mindshare is important

Chen Mian believes that the benefit of being the ‘world's first XX Agent’ is a wave of attention, first-mover traffic, and brand impression. Doing PMF in North America, the hardest part is actually 0 to 1; later it's about product strength, but how product strength reaches the public eye is very important. In the 0 to 1 stage, attention is limited and product competition is fierce, so you need to reach the public in some way. Manus being the first to do a general agent is meaningful, proving that technology has given the conditions for product realization. The first and second may not differ much in experience and technology, but people will only remember the first. Lovart is the first in the vertical, but the true first in the vertical is Cursor; they want to be the Cursor of design, the Cursor of creation.

— Chen Mian
1:07:30

Product managers are useless; you only need people who teach AI industry knowledge

Chen Mian said that in the know-how of AI application startups, product managers really aren't used much; industry data and industry cognition are very important. In terms of organizational form, the most core now are people who teach AI industry knowledge and people who can code: if someone who can code can also teach AI industry knowledge, then you only need people who can code; if everyone can code, then you only need people who teach AI industry knowledge. The original product managers are deconstructed, and the R&D team is also simplified—no need for front-end, back-end, or testing, only R&D who can use AI coding. The team is now close to 100 people, but the second-generation product has very few people, mainly in San Francisco.

— Chen Mian
1:24:04

Coexisting with anxiety, no time to be anxious—just do it

Chen Mian said he is a combative person, and when he gets excited he gets very excited. When asked how he adjusts his mindset, he said there's no time to be anxious, just do it. Coexisting with anxiety cannot be solved, and he doesn't want to solve it—if he weren't so anxious, he wouldn't be so sensitive, unable to judge many problems. He needs a high degree of anxiety to stay sensitive, thereby maintaining sharpness in business judgment and business plans, and a hunger for execution speed. The happiest moment is when what he thought would happen in the future actually happens, and he actually achieves it, such as when Lovart's product form received good feedback. He scrolled through every post on Xiaohongshu, every user feedback, and concluded that this form should be right.

— Chen Mian

Glossary

PMF / Product-Market Fit
The product finds a market that truly wants to use it continuously; it's the hardest 0-to-1 stage in early startups.
agentic / Agentic Capability
The model's ability to autonomously plan, call tools, and complete multi-step tasks.
workflow / Workflow
A way for users to manually break tasks into nodes and connect them into a process for execution.
break even / Break Even
The critical point where revenue covers costs and losses stop.
