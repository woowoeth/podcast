The robotics industry is not romantic: the chain is too long, you have to stick your head into the dirt

Xinghaitu founder Gao Jiyang says robotics startup competition is a hexagon: whole machine, supply chain, data, model, channel, terminal. The algorithm propagation cycle is only two to three months, the smallest moat.

33:46

Waymo's problem is not technology, it's having no founder

Gao Jiyang joined Waymo in early 2019. When he left, the perception team had grown from about ten people to seventy or eighty, and the company from a thousand to nearly two thousand. He judged Waymo had two problems: first, it was too afflicted by big-company disease, with all systems aligned to Google, entering big-company status before creating value; second, big-company disease was only the surface — the essence was that Waymo had no Founder. Its Founder was Google's Founder, but Google's Founder had no time to directly manage this, so the top-down force was missing. He said that in this kind of industry, being wrong is not what you fear; what you fear is force that is not concentrated and not unified.

— Gao Jiyang
44:34

Four autonomous driving business models, Huawei is the fourth

Gao Jiyang broke autonomous driving business models into four types: the first is Waymo's robotaxi, operating its own fleet and charging per ride; the second is car companies selling cars and making autonomous driving a software subscription; the third is suppliers like Momenta, providing solutions to car companies with NRE plus License; the fourth is Huawei, between car company and supplier, essentially earning profit at the whole-vehicle level — redefining the car with top-tier autonomous driving experience and cockpit experience, plus its own brand and channels. He believes Waymo's business model itself has no problem, it's just that the cycle is very long, and today one can be said to see the dawn of it working.

— Gao Jiyang
1:08:42

Leaving Momenta to start a company, gave up over ten million dollars

Gao Jiyang left Momenta in May 2023. Before that he had done perception, localization and parking systems, infra, planning and control at M, and his last job was mass-production delivery of the high-speed high-price NV system to SAIC. He said leaving M to start a company gave up all the completeness; calculated at that point in time, ten million dollars was probably there. Asked if his heart ached, he said not at all, because compared with what he wanted to do, other things had little value. On his thirtieth birthday at the end of 2022 he figured out he wanted to start a company. The trigger was GPT-3 and InstructGPT making the world believe in AI again, and mass-production autonomous driving bringing up the sensor and compute supply chain for edge intelligence.

— Gao Jiyang
1:23:55

The first BP was unbearable to look at, angel round 30 million RMB

Gao Jiyang started putting together the BP in August 2023. Looking back at that BP, he said it was "simply unbearable to look at." The earliest idea was terminal delivery robots, quickly rejected by himself. The first round of financing was 30 million RMB, led by IDG with Baidu Ventures and GSR Ventures following, pre- or post-money valuation 200 million RMB; immediately after, Teacher Wang's CFound fund did an extension round, investing ten or twenty million, post-money valuation three or four hundred million. He said at the time investors clearly said "the thing you want to do should not work, think it over again" — that is angel: accepting your mistakes and imperfections.

— Gao Jiyang
1:35:26

Doing the whole machine first, because the data closed loop must have a carrier

Gao Jiyang explained why the first thing in starting a company was to tackle the most unfamiliar whole machine: long-term moats are built on the data closed loop of the physical world, and the data closed loop must have a carrier, and that carrier is the whole machine hardware; the product to be delivered in the medium and short term is also not an algorithm or a brain, but a physical entity formed by the whole machine plus intelligence, with execution capability in the physical world. So reasoning backward from both the long term and the medium-short term, the whole machine and supply chain must be done well first. The theme of the company for the whole of 2024 was the whole machine and supply chain, which he called making up for lost lessons.

— Gao Jiyang
1:57:22

The cost of one hour of real data is 200 to 250 RMB

Gao Jiyang did the math: collecting one hour of data in the real world actually takes three to four hours of manpower, plus robot depreciation (calculated at 100,000 RMB, conservatively scrapped at a 1,000-hour lifespan), the cost per hour is about 200 to 250 RMB. He further said the relationship between data acquisition cost and training cost is roughly 1 to 5 to 1 to 10 — data obtained for one yuan must be trained on for five to ten yuan to be understood, so low data quality means wasting money on training. He also said 100,000 hours of data is the total magnitude of a person's interaction with the physical world from birth to eighteen.

— Gao Jiyang
2:03:25

Data recipe is today's biggest secret

Gao Jiyang said data comes in many kinds: robot-centric real-machine teleoperation data, human-centric 5-meter and collection-glove data, POV head-mounted camera data, third-person internet video, and graphics-based or world-model-generated simulation data. But no one knows what the ratio of these data should be. This is called data recipe, and many large model companies' biggest secret today lies here. He clearly said Xinghaitu is mainly based on real data, but whether it is 10,000 hours of real-machine data, 50,000 hours of 5-meter data or 200,000 hours of POV data must be found out by experiment — AI is ultimately an experimental science.

— Gao Jiyang
2:34:51

The algorithm propagation cycle is only two to three months, the smallest moat

Gao Jiyang used "propagation cycle" to measure the moat of each element: whole machine and supply chain are 12 to 18 months, customer channel establishment starts from a cycle and large customers are longer, the data system needs another 6 to 12 months on top of the whole machine, while the algorithm propagation cycle for first-tier companies is only two to three months. So algorithm investment is large but the moat is small; the moat against being copied is very small. From this he explained why the company values are called "pragmatic innovation" — innovation must first be pragmatic, idealism cannot become empty fantasy, and the basis for idealism to be realized is calculating ROI every day.

— Gao Jiyang

Glossary

VLA / vision-language-action model
A foundation model that takes vision and language as input and outputs actions, driving the body to execute tasks.
data recipe
The mixing ratio of different types of data (real machine, 5-meter, POV, simulation), determining model performance.
sim-to-real gap
The distribution difference between simulation data rendered by graphics and real-world data.
domain transfer
Transferring a model trained in one domain to another; Gao Jiyang believes it is better to directly use data from that domain.
NRE plus License
The charging model for suppliers providing autonomous driving solutions to car companies: one-time engineering fee plus authorization fee.
