2B agents are not copies of 2C: enterprises will let AI hack their systems

The Afine founder believes 2C personal agents are oversaturated, competing on supply chains and subsidies; 2B agents more easily capture end-to-end value because bosses actively cooperate with AI to transform their companies.

The argument · tap a timestamp to hear it

7:03

A hundred agents plus a hundred employees amplify noise

When you give each employee a Personal Agent, leadership no longer faces 100 employees but 100 employees plus 100 uncoordinated agents, amplifying noise rather than signal. Their solution is a single agent shared across the company with continuous learning that preserves individual memory and operating environments while learning across all business touchpoints.

— He Jiachen
14:07

2C agents have almost no economies of scale

AI startups differ from internet startups because scale and network effects are greatly weakened—more users doesn't make your model smarter; the model's own intelligence improvement is decisive. This is why they don't build 2C personal agents: that market is about supply chains and subsidies, not product moats.

— He Jiachen
19:13

2B bosses actively let AI hack their own systems

In C2C, getting all user information for recommendations is frightening; agents naturally need tricks and reverse engineering to get permissions. In B2B, when the boss sees that AI can trace end-to-end business data, financial data, and every code flow, the boss has strong incentive to actively transform systems to give AI more context. This makes 2B agents easier to adopt early on.

— He Jiachen
26:20

Workflow plugins have shorter and shorter lifespans

Products like LongChain, Dify, N8N, and Open Cloud that scaffolded workflows once soared in valuation but are barely mentioned by H2 2026. Not because better workflows replaced them, but because models absorbed these workflows—with smarter models, the first move is usually to delete previous skills and system prompts or they backfire. Determinism is shifting from outside the model to inside, through KV Cache and Prompt Cache hit rates.

— He Jiachen
35:31

Building your own models only became viable in 2026

The speaker deeply participated in training a domestic ET-scale multimodal agentic model and didn't believe startups could train models—big tech has vastly superior chips, talent density, and end-to-end infrastructure. But by 2026, with architecture changes (like Jamba models) and base model capabilities reaching threshold, small models don't need to beat AGI models; they just need to efficiently distill tasks they can definitely do at lower cost and faster latency. That makes startup model training viable.

— He Jiachen
36:32

Small models are the autonomic nervous system, large models are the cortex

They train two types of models. Task Routing Models distill the tool-calling patterns and user preferences from SOTA models into ‘muscle memory,’ letting small models form conditioned reflexes like learned bike riding. Worker Models of 20-40 billion parameters handle reverse-engineering and specific tasks at the operating-system level of enterprises, breaking through safety constraints. The intelligent large model is the cortex; their trained small models are the brainstem and spinal cord.

— He Jiachen
40:34

All software today is essentially open source

If anyone knows what your software does and can get the API spec, AI can reverse-engineer it or rewrite an identical replacement. So software itself is dying, and value shifts to model intelligence itself. The speaker predicts that in 2-3 years, open models will catch up much faster, AGI supply will become abundant, and the commercial rules for both B2B and C2C will change very bloodily.

— He Jiachen
43:34

Agents are crossing the inflection point that video once owned

In the past 2-3 years, only chat and video—with predictable delivery and commercial closure—made money early. General agents need end-to-end completion to create value; one missing step means zero value. But that gap recently closed, and the advantages video and companionship tracks accumulated face challenge from general infrastructure and general models. OPUS 5.5 is the best video-editing model, GPT6 Astra the best at robotic video control—a complete reversal.

— He Jiachen

Glossary

Proactive Agent
An agent that not only passively responds to conversation but proactively contacts users and advances tasks.
KV Cache / Prompt Cache
Storage of the model's previously generated results, enabling identical calls to be reused directly without re-inference.
Task Routing Model
A small model that distills the large model's task-completion trajectories and user preferences, replicating them at low cost.
