Consciousness Has No Finish Line: The Brain Is Multiple Drafts, Not a Screening Room

Dennett rejects the ‘Cartesian Theater’: there is no single finish line where all sensations converge into consciousness. The brain is a set of parallel discriminations that never get totaled up — and even the question of when you became aware of something often has no factual answer.

Mental events follow no laws, yet reasons can still be causes that leave us free

Davidson argues that mental events fall under no strict laws and are identical to physical events only as particulars; but in the explanation of action, reasons are genuinely causes, and when a reason causes an action the agent is precisely then free.

Being Itself Has No Content: Peirce Overturns Substance-Centrism in Nine Pages

In a nine-page paper from 1868, Peirce takes ‘a concept must be able to unify the content of consciousness’ as his test, deriving being from substance and then the intermediate categories such as quality. Being itself has no content; it only connects a predicate to a subject.
