Water privatisation is not destiny: Uruguay's referendum on the right to water

On 31 October 2004, Uruguayans voted 64.7% in favour of writing ‘water is a natural resource essential to life’ into the constitution, and of restricting water supply to the state alone — making Uruguay the first country in the world to lock water privatisation out at constitutional level.
