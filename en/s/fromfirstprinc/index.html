The quantum endgame isn't about cleaner qubits — it's about who can mass-produce on a phone-chip line

The endgame in quantum computing is not whose qubits are cleanest, but who can mass-produce on existing semiconductor lines: silicon spin reuses TSMC's lithography, while trapped ions are stuck on a hundred thousand laser beams and superconductors on warehouse-sized refrigerators.
