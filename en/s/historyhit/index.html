WWI air combat wasn't romantic — it was a meat grinder with a two-week life expectancy

When the war began, aircraft were still a joke to the officer class. In four years the sky became a meat grinder of reconnaissance, bombing and dogfighting, and by 1917 a new pilot's average life expectancy was roughly two weeks.
