The DeLorean Case: Industrial Policy Should Never Bet on a Single Firm

DeLorean built its factory on roughly $100 million in British government support, then slid into an $18 million fraud and an FBI cocaine sting. The real lesson of industrial policy is not whether to subsidize, but to keep several firms competing, use export markets as discipline, and never bet on one company.
