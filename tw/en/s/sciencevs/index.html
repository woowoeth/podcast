Science Vs 0827

The sunscreen-is-toxic case falls apart: entering the bloodstream is not the same as harm — but the vitamin D risk is real

The evidence most often cited for ‘sunscreen is toxic, sunscreen causes cancer’ mostly involves absurd doses or reversed causation; chemical filters showing up in blood does not make them poisonous. But daily use does raise the risk of winter vitamin D deficiency, and the evidence that sunscreen prevents melanoma in people with darker skin is especially thin.
